# Sales Data Analysis Using Power BI

## Project Objective
- Clean and prepare the raw sales dataset for analysis.
- Identify and handle missing values.
- Detect and remove duplicate entries.
- Standardize inconsistent date formats.
- Analyze customer orders, sales, profit, products, and costs.
- Create meaningful KPIs to measure business performance.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for data visualization and analysis.

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
- DAX – KPI and measure creation
- Power BI Visualizations – Data analysis and dashboard development

## Data Cleaning & Transformation
The following data preparation activities were performed:
- Identified and handled missing values.
- Detected and removed duplicate records.
- Standardized inconsistent date formats.
- Corrected data types where required.
##Insight
Overall Sales Performance: Total sales are 89.96M, with total cost of 85.01M and total profit of 4.94M
Regional Sales: The East region has the highest sales, followed by South, North, and West.
Category Performance: Furniture contributes slightly more sales than Electronics with approximately 46M and 44M respectively
Product Performance: Monitor is the highest-selling product, followed by Cabinet. Several other products have relatively similar sales levels.
Sales Trend: Sales fluctuate considerably across the years, with several noticeable peaks and drops.
Profit vs. Sales: Although sales are high at 89.96M, profit is comparatively small at 4.94M.
Order Volume: The dashboard shows 959 total orders and approximately 3K total quantity sold.
 ##Recommendation
Improve Profitability: Review pricing, supplier costs, discounts, and operating expenses to increase the profit margin.
Strengthen High-Performing Regions: Maintain strong customer engagement in the East while developing targeted promotions to increase sales in lower-performing regions.
Optimize Both Categories: Continue promoting Furniture while identifying high-demand Electronics products and improving their sales contribution.
Focus on High-Selling Products: Maintain sufficient stock of top-performing products and use cross-selling or bundle offers to increase revenue.
Analyze Seasonal Patterns: Investigate the causes of sales peaks and declines and plan inventory, promotions, and marketing campaigns around high-demand periods.
Control Costs: Identify products, regions, or periods with low margins and take measures such as supplier negotiation, pricing optimization, and reducing unnecessary costs.
Increase Order Value: Introduce product bundles, complementary-product recommendations, and volume discounts to increase the quantity and value per order.
